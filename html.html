Interdisciplinary Platforms for Biology, Chemistry, and Engineering

We integrate synthetic biology, material science, chemistry, and engineering approaches for sustainable synthesis and remediation. We apply multiple bio/analytical platforms and data analytics to study environmental impacts on human populations and ecosystems.

Immediate Openings: We have openings for post-docs and graduate students with microbiology, molecular biology, synthetic biology, biochemistry, electrochemistry, or material sciences backgrounds. Please contact the PI.

Research Focus

Synthetic Biology & Electrocatalysis

Integrating chemical catalysis with synthetic biology to produce high-value platform chemicals, fuels, and bioplastics from greenhouse gases.

Emerging Contaminant Bioremediation

Engineering biological and bio/hybrid materials to remove and remediate persistent environmental pollutants such as PFAS and microplastics.

Systems Biology & Analytics

Utilizing multi-omics, bioinformatics, and computational tools to elucidate biological mechanisms and guide sustainable interventions.

Dai Lab Values

The Dai lab strives to think broadly and deeply to implement novel ideas that advance science and serve the local community and the public. We are dedicated to high-quality and reproducible research. Our lab fosters an inclusive and collaborative environment, and we value everyone's unique contribution.
